# Compliance matrix

Status key: **Built** (exists in this repo and is tested), **Designed** (described, not built in the lab), **Process** (people and paperwork, not code), **Verify** (needs checking against the source).

| # | Obligation | What it asks, in plain words | What this design does | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | APRA CPS 230 Operational Risk Management (in force from 1 July 2025) (verify) | Know your critical operations, set tolerance levels, manage service providers, keep business continuity credible, tell APRA about big incidents and material provider deals | Contact centre named as a critical operation; tolerance levels in `dr-and-bcp.md`; provider register template; roll-back and DR rehearsal; incident clocks in `incident-runbook.md` | Register entry; DR rehearsal record; roll-back rehearsal record | Process + Designed |
| 2 | APRA CPS 234 Information Security (verify) | Protect information assets in line with their sensitivity, including those at providers; test controls; notify serious incidents | Least privilege roles, encryption with customer-managed keys, API key + firewall allow-list, no personal data in logs, evidence export, alarms | `terraform/modules/*`, `check_repo.py`, `07-evidence-export` | Built |
| 3 | Privacy Act 1988, Australian Privacy Principles | Collect only what you need, tell people, secure it, limit use and disclosure, allow access and correction | Keypad capture for numbers; fixed-text case summaries; summary rejects card/tax/Medicare numbers; no personal data in logs; recording notice | `test_handlers.py`, `claims_self_service.yaml`, `front_door.yaml` | Built |
| 4 | APP 8 cross-border disclosure (verify) | If personal information goes overseas, you remain accountable | Data stays in Australian regions for storage in this design. The AI model setting warns that cross-region inference profiles may process outside Australia. Where the CXone tenant is hosted and where its AI features process data must be confirmed with the vendor in writing | Variable description for `bedrock_model_id`; vendor questionnaire | Verify |
| 5 | Automated decision-making transparency (APP changes due to commence December 2026) (verify) | If a computer makes or substantially helps make a decision that significantly affects someone, say so in the privacy policy | The AI path only answers general questions from approved text and never decides claims or prices. | 6 | Notifiable Data Breaches scheme | Tell the regulator and affected people about eligible data breaches as soon as practicable | Breach path in `incident-runbook.md`; logs designed to hold no personal data, which also reduces what can leak | `incident-runbook.md` | Process |

## How to use this in an interview

Say what is **built and tested**, what is **designed**, and what needs **checking against the source**. Do not claim a lab proves compliance. A lab shows that you know where the controls go and how to prove they work.
